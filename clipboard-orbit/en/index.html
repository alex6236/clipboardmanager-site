Features

Everything in one popup and a floating window

Automatic saving

Every copy lands in your history on its own: text, links and images from pages.

Search and pin

Instant search across the whole history. Important items stay pinned at the top.

Snippets

Keep frequent phrases, addresses and details apart from the history; date and time are filled in by variables.

Screenshots

Capture the visible area or a selected region. Crop, arrows and notes in the editor.

Floating window

History on top of other windows — handy when the browser fills the screen.

Groups and multi-select

Select several items and pin, move or delete them in one action.

Install

Three steps, under a minute

1

Add it from the catalog

Open the card in Opera Add-ons and press “Add to Opera”. Yandex Browser installs extensions from the Chrome and Opera catalogs.

2

Pin the icon

Click the puzzle-piece button in the toolbar and pin Clipboard Orbit so the icon stays next to the address bar.

3

Copy

The history appears on its own. Open it from the icon or the shortcut, search and paste in one click.

Your data stays with you

History, pins and snippets are stored locally in the browser. The extension does not send clipboard content to a server, does not require an account and works offline.

Privacy policy

Frequently asked

The short version

Where is the clipboard history stored?

Locally: text and settings in browser storage, images in the extension’s local database. Nothing is sent over the network.

Does it sync between devices?

Not in this version. Export and backup are saved as files on disk, so you can move them manually.

Does it work in Yandex Browser?

Yes. Yandex Browser installs extensions from the Chrome and Opera catalogs; this build accounts for its API differences.

How much does it cost?

It is free — no account, no subscription. All features are available right after installation.

Where do screenshots go?

To the browser’s Downloads folder or to the folder chosen in settings. The file name includes the date and time of the capture.
