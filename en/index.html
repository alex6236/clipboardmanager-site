Features

A clipboard manager that stays out of your way and does more than the built-in Win+V.

Text and image history

Every copy is saved automatically — text, pictures and screenshots. Your history survives a Windows restart.

Search, categories, pinning

Instant search across the whole history, folders for clips, and pinning what matters to the top. The category panel collapses, and sections you don't need can be hidden in Settings.

Snippets and templates

Frequent addresses, details and phrases on Ctrl+Shift+Alt+S. Groups and variables in Pro.

Paste as plain text

Strip formatting with Ctrl+Shift+Alt+U and apply text transformations on paste.

Screenshots with an editor

Area, active window or full screen on PrtScr. Arrows, text and blur right in the overlay.

Tray, autostart, 8 languages

Runs quietly in the system tray and starts with Windows. Light and dark themes, localized into 8 languages.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free version?

Yes. Free has no time limit: history, search, categories, images, screenshots, dark theme and 8 languages. No sign-up required.

How is Pro different from Free?

Pro adds snippets and templates with groups, variable substitution, advanced import and export, text transformations on paste and priority support.

Can I try Pro before buying?

Pro is enabled for the first 14 days automatically — no key needed. When the trial ends the app switches to Free and keeps your history and settings.

How do I activate a key?

Open Settings → Activation, paste the key and click Activate. The licence covers one user, is not tied to a specific computer, and works offline without contacting a server.

How do I paste without formatting?

Press Ctrl+Shift+Alt+U — ClipboardManager Plus takes the current clipboard and pastes it as clean text, without fonts, colors or links.

Where is my clipboard history stored?

Locally, in a database file on your computer. The app never sends clipboard content anywhere and works without internet.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit editions. The app is native, without Electron or background services.
